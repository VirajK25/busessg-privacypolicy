Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026 · Applies to the BusesSG Android app and widgets

BusesSG shows live Singapore bus arrivals. This policy explains what the BusesSG Android app (package com.busarrival.sg) does with your information. It applies to the app and its home-screen widgets.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • The developer does not run a server for this app and does not receive, collect or store any of your personal data.
  • Your location is optional, is used only while the app is open, and never leaves your phone.
  • Your saved stops and buses stay on your phone.
  • To show arrivals and maps, the app talks directly to three public services (listed below). They see your IP address and the request itself, like any website would.

1. What the app stores on your device

The app keeps a small amount of data in its own private storage on your phone:

  • the stops and buses you chose to save;
  • the stop you looked at last, so the app can open on it next time.

The home-screen widgets use a copy of your saved stops and buses so they can show the right arrivals. This data is never uploaded by the developer. Android backup is switched off for the app, so it is not copied to cloud backups. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

2. Location

The app asks for location permission only when you use Nearby (for example, by pressing “Use my location” or the refresh-location button). Android may offer you precise or approximate location, and you can choose either.

  • Only while the app is open. The app does not request background location and does not track you.
  • Used on your phone only. Your position is used to sort nearby stops and to work out how far a bus is from a stop. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party, and it is not saved after you close the app.
  • Your choice. The app works without location: you can search for any stop by name or code. You can withdraw permission at any time in Android Settings, under Apps, BusesSG, Permissions.

3. Services the app connects to

The app has no server of its own. To work, it connects directly to these services over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Each receives your IP address and standard technical details that any internet request includes, plus the request itself:

  • LTA DataMall (datamall2.mytransport.sg), run by Singapore’s Land Transport Authority. The app sends the code of the bus stop you are viewing, using an access key issued to the developer, and receives arrival times, bus load and bus positions.
  • ArriveLah (arrivelah2.busrouter.sg), a public bus-arrival service used as a back-up only when LTA DataMall is not available. The app sends the bus stop code.
  • OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org), which provides map styles and map tiles when you open a bus on the map. Map tile requests reveal the map area being shown (around the bus stop and the bus), but the app does not send your location to it.

These services are operated by other organisations and have their own privacy practices, which the developer does not control. The app does not send them your name, email address, device identifiers, advertising ID or your location.

4. Home-screen widgets

Widgets refresh about every 30 minutes, and when you tap refresh, by requesting arrivals for the stop or bus they show from LTA DataMall (or ArriveLah as a back-up), as described in section 3. Widgets do not use your location.

5. Sharing and selling

The developer does not collect your data, so there is nothing to sell, rent, share for advertising, or pass to data brokers. The only transfers are the ones in section 3, which are needed for the app to show arrivals and maps.

6. Permissions the app uses

  • INTERNET – to load arrivals and maps.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION – for the optional Nearby feature, foreground only.

The app does not use your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, calendar or phone number.

7. Security

All connections to the services above use HTTPS, and the app does not allow unencrypted traffic. Because the app collects no accounts or personal data, there is no user database that could be breached. No method of electronic storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we keep the app deliberately simple to limit what could go wrong.

8. Retention and deletion

The developer holds no personal data about you. Data on your phone stays until you remove saved items, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Third-party services keep their own logs under their own policies.

9. Children

BusesSG is a general transport app and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. As the developer holds none, there is nothing to retrieve, but you are welcome to contact us with any question or request.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app’s handling of data changes, this policy will be updated and the date below will change. The current version is always available on this page and inside the app (tap “Privacy policy” at the bottom of the main screen).
